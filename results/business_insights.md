## 4. Key Business Insights

1. Electronics is the leading product category in terms of revenue.
2. Revenue contribution is relatively balanced across the three product categories.
3. Female customers contribute a slightly higher share of total revenue than male customers.
4. May 2023 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
5. Customers aged 46–55 generated the highest revenue among the analyzed age groups.
6. Repeat customers represent an opportunity for customer retention strategies.
7. The top-spending customer made four transactions, indicating repeat purchasing behavior.
